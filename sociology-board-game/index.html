This game does not treat race or identity as ability. Identity changes exposure to social systems, bias, access, representation, and institutional treatment. You still make choices, and chance still matters.

The point is to make structural inequality visible without turning stereotypes into “traits.” A character can face bias because of how institutions or people respond to them—not because their identity makes them inherently better or worse.
