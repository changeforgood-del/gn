HOW JUDGING WORKS

Scores use pose landmarks and transparent rules: extension/elbow path, opposite-hand guard, shoulder–hip rotation, stance stability, wrist velocity, return to guard, and an audio exhale cue.

Research basis & limitations

Benchmarks are informed by peer-reviewed biomechanics—not copied from a single “perfect punch.” See Dinu et al. (2020), Liu et al. (2022), and Ishac & Eager (2021). Individual anatomy and styles vary.

Training aid only. A phone camera cannot measure true bag impact, fist mass, wrist alignment, contact quality, or injury risk. Use a qualified coach and appropriate protective equipment.
