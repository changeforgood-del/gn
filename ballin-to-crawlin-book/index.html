What this book is

A story told from inside the struggle

This site organizes the book as a living narrative rather than flattening it into a conventional memoir. The voice moves between storytelling, prayer, reflection, testimony, song, sermon, memory, and direct conversation. The wording in the reading sections is preserved from the source manuscript and recovered story material.

01 • ROOTS

Grandma’s hands

A fatherless childhood, the boys in the hood, early ideas of morality, and the seeds of faith that stay even before they are understood.

02 • THE GAME

More than what you do

The “game” is described as the environment surrounding hustling: vigilance, identity, money, status, danger, habit, and a state of mind.

03 • THE TURN

From knees to ministry

Family changes the question. Prayer becomes a turning point. Discipline, church, children, and ministry create a new direction.

04 • THE FALL

Preacher in the game

Divorce and old habits reopen the conflict. Faith remains, even while the narrator describes living between two worlds.

05 • THE BIG PICTURE

A place called somewhere

Loss becomes a mental and spiritual landscape: being present but absent, trying to return to destiny, and learning to take necessary steps.

06 • RETURN

Wisdom, praise, solid ground

The later stories focus on trust, obedience, wisdom, understanding, gratitude, praise, and rebuilding a state of mind that can hold.

Book map

The complete arc

The website treats the recovered material as connected episodes in one book-length spiritual autobiography.

Episode 1

Ballin’ to Crawlin’. Childhood, fatherlessness, grandmother, gangs, family, the meaning of “the game,” and the first cry to God.

Episode 2

Preacher in the Game. Ministry, discipline, family success, divorce, relapse, spiritual conflict, and identity as a preacher who has not been abandoned.

Episode 3

The Big Picture. Eight months after losing everything, the narrator describes a “place called somewhere,” altered states, people/places/things, promises, and the desire to reclaim destiny.

Episode 4

High and Low — The Big Picture About Steps. The difference between reaching a better state and maintaining it; faith, trust, human nature, spirituality, and the cost of not taking the next step.

Episode 5

Steps Through the Cross Roads I Know for Myself. Wisdom, understanding, surrender, obedience, scripture, love, and seeing recovery as a sequence of steps.

Episode 6

Thank You Story. Gratitude, mercy, grace, praise, church memory, deliverance, and being set back on solid ground.

Second Story

Praise. A shorter closing movement focused on joy, spiritual strength, laying aside weight, and praising through the fight.

Deep breakdown

The ideas that keep returning

These are organizing themes drawn from the book’s own language and recurring story structure, not replacements for the author’s wording.

Grandmother / planted seedsFatherlessnessThe game as environmentFamily and legacyPrayerMinistryDivorce and relapsePreacher in the gameA place called somewhereDestinyNecessary stepsTrustWisdomUnderstandingObedienceMercyGracePraiseSolid ground
Pattern 1

Up → down → middle → movement. The book repeatedly measures life as states or positions and asks what steps are required to move or remain.

Pattern 2

Environment matters. “The game” is repeatedly described not just as an act but as a surrounding environment that can become more addictive than the act itself.

Pattern 3

Identity survives failure. The preacher identity remains present through backsliding, creating the central tension of being “in the game” without fully belonging to it.

Pattern 4

Faith becomes method. Later chapters move from testimony toward a practical framework: take steps, change environment, trust, seek wisdom, learn obedience, praise, repeat.
